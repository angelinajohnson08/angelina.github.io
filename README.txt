# The Green Table — student website project

## Files
- index.html — home
- products.html — menu with category filters and select_item tracking
- about.html — brand story
- contact.html — demonstration contact/newsletter forms
- style.css — responsive styling
- script.js — navigation, filters, validation and GA4 events

## Before publishing
Replace every occurrence of `G-XXXXXXXXXX` in all four HTML files with your GA4 Measurement ID.
The contact and newsletter forms are demos. They validate locally but do not send or store data.
Images are loaded from Unsplash URLs and Google Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so those assets
require an internet connection. The email, phone, address and menu prices are illustrative sample content.
